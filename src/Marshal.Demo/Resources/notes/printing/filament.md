# Filament settings

| Material | Hotend | Bed | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| PLA | 210 °C | 60 °C | Door open, fan 100 % |
| PETG | 240 °C | 80 °C | Dry 4 h at 65 °C first; fan 40 % |
| ASA | 255 °C | 105 °C | Enclosure closed, warm up 15 min |
